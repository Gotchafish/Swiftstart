How Swift Start will work

  1. Customer chooses a company service and submits a secure request
  2. Swift Start checks information and quotes government fees separately
  3. Customer reviews and authorises the filing
  4. Authorised staff submit via approved channels and record the reference
  5. Customer receives confirmation and future compliance reminders

Roadmap: secure accounts, per-company permissions, audit logs, encrypted documents, official-source verification and opt-in email notifications. No government integration is implied.
